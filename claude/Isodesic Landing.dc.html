About

Nearly twenty years designing gear people carry up mountains.

I have spent my career inside the outdoor industry, drawing tents, pads, chairs, and packs that had to hit a weight target, a price, and a ship date at the same time. That work taught me how to make decisions early, where the real constraints live, and how to talk to a factory.

Isodesic is a practice of one, which means you work directly with the person doing the design. I take on outdoor projects and technical hardgoods well beyond it.
