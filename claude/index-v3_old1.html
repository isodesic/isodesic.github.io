02 / SERVICES

What I can do for you

Most clients hire me end to end, from an open question about their line to production samples that match the spec. Every contract is scoped to where you're starting: a gap in the market, a half-formed idea, or a finished concept that just needs a tech pack.

PRODUCT DESIGN

Research & strategy

Know there's a hole in your line but not what should fill it? I survey the competitive landscape, study how the category actually gets used, and set clear targets for weight, cost, and features.

PRODUCT DESIGN

Concept design

A broad round of ideation, narrowed to a handful of concepts. Tents and shelters are modeled with my own tools that simulate bending poles and tensioned fabric. Have an idea that isn't fully baked? I'll flesh it out.

PRODUCT DESIGN

Tech packs

Detailed, factory-ready tech packs and BOMs for a first sample, built from my concepts or from a design you bring me. Clear enough that the factory isn't left guessing.

PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

Samples to production

I work directly with your factory to get samples made: spec'ing materials, trims, and colors, reviewing each round, and updating the tech pack, all the way to approving production samples against the spec.

PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

Sourcing & factory introductions

Introductions to vetted tent, sleeping pad, fabric, and trim suppliers across Asia from a network built over nearly twenty years, plus quote and BOM reviews against your cost targets.

ENGINEERING

Custom trims & hardware

Clamps, pole connectors, stakes, buckles, and grips engineered in SolidWorks for injection molding, stamping, laser cutting, or 3D printing — small parts that set a product apart.

03 / PROCESS

What my typical project looks like

01

Research the gap

Market and competitive research, needfinding, and clear targets for weight, cost, and use. We agree on what success looks like before any design starts.

02

Model it realistically

Several architectures in 3D, with poles and fabric that behave like the real thing, so tradeoffs show before anything is sewn. You choose from a handful of concepts, each with the numbers behind it.

03

Spec and sample

Tech packs, a factory introduction if you need one, then prototype rounds reviewed and revised in detail. Every round ends with written feedback and an updated tech pack.

04

Carry it to production

Materials, trims, and colors locked in, and production samples checked against the spec so what ships is what we designed. One point of contact for you and the factory the whole way.

05 / EXPERT WITNESS

Patent litigation support

Attorneys retain me as a technical expert in utility and design patent cases involving tents, portable furniture, and other fabric-based products. I bring the view of someone who has designed and commercialized 250+ of them, holds two U.S. design patents, and knows how these products are actually engineered and made.

Subject matter

  • Tents, shelters, and pole-supported or tensioned-fabric structures
  • Folding and portable furniture, including camp chairs
  • Backpacks, luggage, and travel goods
  • Sleeping pads, air mattresses, and other welded or inflatable products
  • Sewn and fabric-based consumer products

Services

  • Infringement and validity analysis
  • Claim construction
  • Prior art search and analysis
  • Design-around and non-infringing alternatives
  • Expert reports and deposition testimony

Recent engagement: retained on behalf of the plaintiff in a federal patent infringement case (C.D. Cal.) involving portable camp chairs. Authored three expert reports on claim construction, infringement, and validity, offering opinions as both a person of ordinary skill in the art (POSITA) and a designer of ordinary skill (DOSA); deposed on claim construction report.

Available nationwide, remote or on site.

06 / ABOUT

Who you'd be working with

I design gear because I'm the one out there using it.

My path here wasn't a straight line. After studying product design at Stanford, a program built on needfinding and hands-on prototyping, I wrote software in Silicon Valley, hand-coded websites, and spent a year building high-end speaker cabinets in a Boulder woodshop. Code and craft both turned out to matter more than I expected.

Tents pulled me into the outdoor industry. I spent four years as The North Face's lead tent designer, taking prototypes to athletes like Conrad Anker and Alex Honnold, then left to travel solo for most of a year: 66,000 miles through 12 countries, from Nepal to New Zealand to South America, testing gear and photographing along the way. After that came more than six years at Big Agnes, where I owned the ultralight tent line and designed 200+ products, from the Copper Spur and Tiger Wall to camp chairs and sleeping pads.

I started Isodesic in Bozeman, Montana, in 2021. It's a practice of one, so the person you hire is the person modeling your product, writing your tech pack, and reviewing every sample. When I'm not at the computer, I'm usually outside: backpacking, hiking, trying out a prototype, or behind a camera.
