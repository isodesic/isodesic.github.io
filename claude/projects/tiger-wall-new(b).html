Tiger Wall

A family of award-winning, ultralight backpacking tents with the highest space-to-weight ratio for a semi-freestanding architecture

Big Agnes needed a tent that filled the gap between two of its own icons: the minimalist Fly Creek UL and the roomier but heavier Copper Spur UL. I surveyed the competition and worked from sketches to pipe cleaner models to full-scale pole sets and six rounds of sampling, landing on a high hub and a wide ridge pole that buy a lot of livable volume for very little weight, plus a side door for each occupant. The Tiger Wall UL2 and UL3 shipped for Spring 2018 and grew into a 23-project family — Platinum, Carbon, mtnGLO, EX, Bikepack, and a solution-dyed Spring '21 update — with awards from the Outdoor Show, Backpacker, and GearLab.

Big Agnes already owned two icons at opposite ends of the ultralight range: the minimalist Fly Creek UL, semi-freestanding on a single-hubbed pole with one front door, and the full-featured Copper Spur UL, freestanding on a dual-hubbed pole with two side doors. The brief, in the summer of 2016, was a hybrid — the lightest architecture that still fit two side-entry doors and enough volume to live in.

I surveyed the semi-freestanding competition, which either weighed too much or gave up too much usable volume, then worked from sketches to mini pipe cleaner models to full-scale pole sets, measuring section lengths as a proxy for weight. Three architectures went to our partner in China for patternmaking and sampling; one stood out, and five more rounds took it to production.

The final design sits closer to the Fly Creek UL, but pre-bent sections near the hub and a wide ridge pole open up the middle so two people can sit up at once. The key insight was hub position: placing it high on the structure bought a lot of usable volume for the weight of a few extra sections on the head-end legs. Doors anchor off the ridge pole tips so either occupant can get out independently, and 15D ripstop throughout balanced weight, cost, and durability.

The UL2 and UL3 filled a gaping hole in the market and quickly took off. The line grew to include the lighter Platinum and Carbon versions, Europe-specific EX models, mtnGLO models with internal LED lights, and Bikepack models for bike touring. For Spring '21 I updated the UL with solution-dyed fabric, custom TipLok pole and buckle attachments, patented Dirt Dagger UL stakes, and a large 3D bin pocket.
