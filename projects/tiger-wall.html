Big Agnes needed a 2-door tent with the livability of a Copper Spur UL but the weight closer to a Fly Creek UL. I experimented with different single-hub architectures and landed on a high hub plus wide ridge pole that offers significant usable space for minimal weight. The UL2 and UL3 were released in 2018 and grew into a 23-SKU family, including Platinum, Carbon, mtnGLO, EX (Europe-specific), and Bikepack versions, with a solution-dyed fabric update to the UL in 2021.

This is the first two-person tent we’ve tested that sneaks in below 2 pounds and doesn’t come with a host of caveats.
